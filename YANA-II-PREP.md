# You Are Not Alone II: prep notes

Written 26 Sep 2026, the night of YANA I. Likely date: December 2026 (not fixed).

## What worked at YANA I (keep)
- Printed name badges, table name cards and signing-in sheet (Kall Kwik Lewisham, about £100 all in).
- QR codes on the table cards: six book pre-orders in two minutes, 15:32 to 15:34.
- "Join ADI tonight" table card with QR codes to /pay/annual and /pay/monthly.
- Nine-song playlist, played in order from the laptop (Oatlands Park has a music licence).
- Google Slides deck in ADI brand fonts (never .pptx).
- Our own Stripe checkout from 25 Sep. The old GHL funnel failed bank checks (SCA) and lost sales.

## Numbers from YANA I
- 29 reservations. 17 people came plus one child (Chizaram Umelo, free).
- 11 no-shows: Elga, Aurelie, Tara, Gelson, Kaddy, Shola, Presh, Linda, Maisie, Rachael, Venita.
  Elga and Tara had paid. Most of the rest never paid.
- Every payment was at £24.99. The £49.99 price sold nothing in six days.

## Do differently for YANA II
- Take payment at booking. Free reservations became no-shows.
- Send paid guests and members a reminder the week before and the day before.
  In YANA I only unpaid guests were ever emailed.
- Sort the WhatsApp Community invite link before the day.
- Photos: ask for photo permission on the booking form, so pictures can be used straight away.
- Give the hotel the final headcount on time. For YANA I it was unconfirmed on the due date.
- Kids go free: say so on the page.
